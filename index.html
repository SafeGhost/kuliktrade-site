How we build

Four promises in every app.

01

Private by design

No account unless the app truly needs one. Your cards, clients and photos stay on your device. We don't sell data or track you across apps.

02

Useful in a minute

Open it, do the job, put the phone away. If something takes more than a few taps, we redesign it.

03

Honest pricing

A real free plan, clear prices before you pay, and subscriptions you can cancel in two taps in your store settings.

04

People answer

Write to us and a person reads it. We usually reply within two business days, in English, Spanish or Russian.
