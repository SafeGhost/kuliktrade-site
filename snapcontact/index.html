Why we made it

Phone numbers live on paper and photos. They should live in your Contacts.

The problem

You take a picture of a plumber's flyer or a business card "for later". Weeks later it is lost among hundreds of photos, and typing a name, two phone numbers, an email and an address by hand takes minutes and goes wrong.

Our answer

Point the camera or share a screenshot. SnapContact reads the text, understands who it is, finds the exact address and the business's logo, and gives you a clean card to check and save with one tap.

What makes it different

More than a card scanner.

It knows who it is

A dentist, a sushi place, an electrician, a salon or a person. Each card gets the right category and a fitting picture.

Not only business cards

Door signs, menus, flyers, storefronts, and screenshots of websites, map listings and profiles.

Exact address and logo

The address and map pin come from Apple Maps, the logo from the business's own website.

Sorted by itself

Nine colorful folders: Health, Food & Drink, Home Services, Auto, Beauty & Fitness and more. Search "dentist" or "sushi" later.

Fast from anywhere

Share from a screenshot or Photos, widgets on the Home and Lock Screen, a Control Center button, the Action Button and Siri.

Private by design

Text is read and understood on your iPhone. Your photos are never uploaded. No account, no ads, no tracking.

How it works

Three steps, a few seconds.

Snap or share

Take a photo in the app, or tap Share › SnapContact on any screenshot or photo.

Check the card

Name, phones, email, website, address and hours are filled in. Fix anything you like.

Save

The contact lands in your iPhone's Contacts, in the right group, with a picture.

Privacy

Your photos stay on your phone

  • Text recognition happens on your iPhone
  • No account and no sign-up
  • No ads and no tracking
  • Only online lookups: Apple Maps and the business's own website for its logo, and you can turn both off
