What to put on a painting estimate

  • Your business name, phone and license number, so the client can call you back and trust you.
  • Each service on its own line with quantity, unit and rate. Clients accept clear lines faster than one big number.
  • Minimum charge, travel fee and materials as separate lines.
  • Deposit amount and when the rest is due.
  • Short terms: how long the price is valid and what happens if the job changes.

A tip for painting

Measure square feet of wall, not of floor, and list materials separately, so the client understands why two rooms cost differently.
