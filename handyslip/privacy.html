HandySlip

Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026 · Kulik Trade Corp

In short: HandySlip does not collect your data. There is no account, no HandySlip server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your phone unless you choose to send it.

What the app stores, and where

Your business profile, services and prices, clients, estimates, invoices, your logo and client signatures are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. We (Kulik Trade Corp) never receive them.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Contacts — only when you tap "From contacts", to fill in a client's name, phone, email and address. The app reads only the contact you pick and does not upload your contacts.
  • Photos — only to choose your business logo.
  • Microphone and speech recognition — only while voice input is on. Speech is turned into text by your phone's own speech service (Apple on iPhone, Google on Android), which may process audio on the device or on Apple's or Google's servers under their privacy policies. HandySlip receives only the resulting text, uses it on the device to find your services, and does not send it anywhere.
  • Notifications — reminders about unpaid invoices and, if you set up recurring jobs, on the morning of each visit. They are scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent to us.

You can say no to any permission; the rest of the app keeps working.

The app also reads your phone's region setting, on the device only, to start with the usual currency and units (feet or meters) for your country.

Sending estimates and invoices

When you send a PDF by text message, email or another app, it goes through that app to the people you choose. If you turn on "Attach my business card", a contact card with the details from your business profile goes along. What you send, and to whom, is up to you.

Backups

The app keeps a backup copy on your phone. Your phone's own backup (iCloud Backup on iPhone, Google backup on Android) may include it, under your Apple or Google account settings. Backup files you save yourself go wherever you choose to put them; you can protect them with a password (AES-256 encryption), which only you know and which cannot be recovered. We have no access to any of these.

Purchases

HandySlip Pro is sold through the App Store and Google Play. Apple or Google handles the payment; we never see your payment details. The app only receives confirmation from the store of what your store account owns.

Children

HandySlip is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes everything it stored on your phone. Copies you saved elsewhere, and your phone's own backups, are managed by you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
