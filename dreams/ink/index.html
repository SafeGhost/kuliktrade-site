Ink in a dream

You spill ink on your clothes.

What it means

Envious people will play petty tricks on you. Do not take them to heart.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Ink on your fingers: do not give in to jealousy or the urge to hurt someone.
  • Red ink: a serious trouble.
  • Bottles of ink: rivals and failed plans.
  • A young woman seeing ink: a rival may slander her.
