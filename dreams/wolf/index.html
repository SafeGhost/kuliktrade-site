A wolf in a dream

You see a wolf.

What it means

There is a dishonest person near you who may give away your secrets.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Killing a wolf means you will defeat sly opponents.
  • Hearing a wolf howl means rivals are secretly plotting against you.
