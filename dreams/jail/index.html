A jail in a dream

You see people in jail.

What it means

You will be pushed to grant favours to people you do not trust. Do not give in against your will.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • A young woman sees her loved one in jail: he may not be who he seems.
  • A jailer: betrayal at work.
  • A mob storming a jail: someone will try to squeeze money out of you.
