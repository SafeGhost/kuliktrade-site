Snakes in a dream

You see snakes.

What it means

Hard times and ill-wishers lie ahead; watch the people around you.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Killing a snake: you will beat your enemies and seize your chance.
  • A snake bites you: bad influences may harm your work.
  • Walking over snakes: worry, and others trying to take your place beside your partner.
  • For a woman, bitten by a dead snake: beware the spite of a false friend.
