Hands in a dream

You see beautiful hands.

What it means

Recognition and quick progress in your field lie ahead.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Washing your hands: a joyful celebration.
  • Seeing your hands grown large: quick advancement.
  • Blood on your hands: a falling-out with relatives.
  • Tied hands: difficulties; untying them, you'll gain the upper hand.
  • Holding fire without getting burned: a high position.
