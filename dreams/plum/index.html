A plum in a dream

You see ripe plums.

What it means

Joyful occasions are coming, though they will not last long.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Green plums off the tree — minor discomfort.
  • Eating plums — light flirting and fleeting pleasures.
  • Picking plums — you will get what you want, but it will be less solid than you thought.
  • Finding rotten ones among the good — life is not made of pleasure alone.
