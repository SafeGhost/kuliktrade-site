A lake in a dream

You sail across a clear, calm lake.

What it means

Good company, happiness and enough money will be yours.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • A rough, muddy lake means changes and regret over past extravagance.
  • A muddy lake among bare rocks means letdowns in work and love.
  • Your reflection in a clear lake means joys and loyal friends.
  • Slimy creatures rising from the water warn you not to waste your strength and health on shady pleasures.
