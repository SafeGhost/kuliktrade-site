A goat in a dream

You see goats grazing near a farm.

What it means

Fine weather and a good harvest: your wealth will steadily grow.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • A billy goat butts you: keep your secrets and plans away from rivals.
  • Drinking goat's milk: a practical marriage that won't disappoint.
