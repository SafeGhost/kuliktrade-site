A sweetheart in a dream

You see your sweetheart.

What it means

If they're warm and attractive in the dream, your choice will bring you joy and pride.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • If they look different than usual, doubts may arise before the wedding.
  • If they're ill or sad, joy will be mixed with sorrow.
