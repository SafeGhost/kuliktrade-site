A tick in a dream

You see a tick on your skin.

What it means

Guard your health and your money, and don't waste your strength.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Crushing a tick means treacherous enemies will annoy you.
  • Ticks on livestock mean someone is dishonestly eyeing your property.
