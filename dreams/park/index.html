A park in a dream

You walk through a well-kept park.

What it means

Pleasant leisure awaits you.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Walking in the park with someone you love promises a happy, peaceful married life.
  • A neglected park with no grass or leaves warns of unexpected setbacks.
