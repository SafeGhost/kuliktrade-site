A shark in a dream

You see sharks.

What it means

You have powerful enemies; be careful.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • A shark chasing you: setbacks and low spirits; hold on.
  • Sharks playing in clear water: amid your success, someone's jealousy is growing.
  • A dead shark: reconciliation and a fresh rise.
