An uncle in a dream

You see your uncle.

What it means

Some less than cheerful news will soon arrive.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • A downcast uncle in a repeated dream points to a rift with relatives, though not for good.
  • Quarrelling with your uncle means tension in the family; look after your health and peace at home.
