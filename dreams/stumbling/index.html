Stumbling in a dream

You stumble.

What it means

Obstacles will rise on your path to success, but you'll get past them as long as you don't fall.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Tumbling off something means you should be more focused and stop putting things off.
  • Seeing others tumble means you'll gain from their carelessness.
