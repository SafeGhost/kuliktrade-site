An accordion in a dream

You hear accordion music.

What it means

Cheerful fun will lift you out of sadness, and your load will feel lighter.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • For a young woman, playing the accordion — love will come through a sad event but bring lasting happiness.
  • An out-of-tune accordion — worry about a loved one.
