Hell in a dream

You find yourself in hell.

What it means

Temptations are coming that could cost you dearly. Do not give in to easy money or dubious pleasures.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Seeing friends in hell: heavy worries and bad news about someone you know.
  • Crying in hell: friends will not be able to pull you out of a trap, so rely on yourself.
