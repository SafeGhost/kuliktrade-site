A duck in a dream

You see wild ducks on clear water.

What it means

Lucky journeys, perhaps even overseas.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Ducks in flight promise a brighter future, sometimes a wedding and children.
  • White ducks around the house mean plenty.
  • Hunting ducks warns that plans at work may go awry.
