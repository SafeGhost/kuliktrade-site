A pit in a dream

You look into a deep pit.

What it means

You risk getting into a foolish venture and worry about love.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Falling into a pit — a hard stretch and grief.
  • Waking up as you start to fall — you will get out of trouble in fairly good shape.
  • Climbing down on purpose — you will risk health and money for greater success.
