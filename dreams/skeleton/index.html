A skeleton in a dream

You see a skeleton.

What it means

Look after your health and beware of enemies: misunderstandings and hurt are possible.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • You are a skeleton yourself: you wear yourself out with needless worry; be gentler with yourself.
  • A skeleton haunting you: a sudden shock or money trouble.
