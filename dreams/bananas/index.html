Bananas in a dream

You see bananas.

What it means

A dull person who does not interest you may end up by your side; do not agree to a relationship out of pity.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Eating bananas — a tiring job you took on yourself.
  • Rotten bananas — do not get drawn into an unpleasant scheme.
  • Selling bananas — effort without profit.
