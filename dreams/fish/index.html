A fish in a dream

You see fish in clear water.

What it means

Fish in clear water mean the support of influential people.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Catching fish while wading: wealth earned by your own work.
  • Eating fish: a warm and lasting attachment.
  • Dead fish: losses, so avoid risking money.
  • Fishing and catching nothing: your efforts won't pay off yet.
