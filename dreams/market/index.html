A market in a dream

You walk through a busy market.

What it means

Thrift and lively activity in all your affairs lie ahead.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • An empty market means gloom and slow times.
  • Spoiled vegetables or meat mean losses at work.
  • For a young woman, a market promises pleasant changes.
