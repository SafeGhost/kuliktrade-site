A funeral in a dream

You are at a funeral on a sunny day.

What it means

Your loved ones will be well, and there may soon be a wedding in the family.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • A funeral in rain and gloomy weather means bad news from afar and slow business.
  • Sad rites and grieving faces point to difficult surroundings.
