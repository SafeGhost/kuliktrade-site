A parcel in a dream

A parcel is delivered to you.

What it means

The return of someone long absent, or unexpected care, will pleasantly surprise you.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Carrying a parcel yourself means you will have an unpleasant task to do.
  • Dropping a parcel on the way warns that some deal will fall through.
