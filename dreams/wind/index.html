The wind in a dream

You walk against a strong wind.

What it means

You will resist temptation and pursue success with determination.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Wind carrying you against your will points to setbacks at work and in love.
  • Wind carrying you where you want to go brings unexpected allies.
  • Hearing the wind moan means longing for someone who misses you.
