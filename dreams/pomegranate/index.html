A pomegranate in a dream

You see a pomegranate.

What it means

You will wisely use your talents to grow your mind rather than chase harmful pleasures.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Your sweetheart gives you a pomegranate — someone’s charm will turn your head, but inner strength will keep you safe.
  • Eating one — you will surrender to another person’s charm.
