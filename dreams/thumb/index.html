A thumb in a dream

You see a thumb.

What it means

Cunning people will be around you, and luck will be fickle. Be careful.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • A sore thumb means losses and unpleasant company.
  • A missing thumb means loneliness.
  • A very large thumb means quick, brilliant success.
