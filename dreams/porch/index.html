A porch in a dream

You stand on a porch.

What it means

You will take on something new, and the future is still full of unknowns.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Building a porch — new duties.
  • Sitting on a porch with your beloved — doubts about someone’s intentions.
