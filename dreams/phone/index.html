A phone in a dream

You see a telephone.

What it means

Strangers may confuse you and get in the way of your affairs. Don't give in to pressure.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Talking on the phone means rivals, but you'll come out on top.
  • Not hearing the other person well means gossip and a chill in a relationship.
