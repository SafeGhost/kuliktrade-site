A lock in a dream

You see a lock on a door.

What it means

Confusion lies ahead.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • If the lock opens easily, you will learn who is working against you, beat a rival and make a profitable trip.
  • If it won't open, expect mockery in love and pointless journeys.
  • Putting a lock on your partner means you doubt their faithfulness, but time will clear your doubts.
