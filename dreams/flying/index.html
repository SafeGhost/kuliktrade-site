Flying in a dream

You are flying in your dream.

What it means

Flying on white wings above green trees means success in work and love.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Seeing the sun while flying: your worries are needless, things will work out.
  • Flying low over the ground: a little tiredness that will soon pass.
  • Flying over muddy water: keep your affairs private.
  • Falling while flying: a setback, but if you wake as you fall, you'll rise again.
