Being abandoned in a dream

You are left all alone.

What it means

It will be hard to make plans for the future; you will need to find your footing again.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • You abandon someone yourself — troubles will pile up around you.
  • Leaving your home — a risky bet will go badly.
  • Abandoning a ship but reaching the shore — your affairs will stay safe.
