An acrobat in a dream

You watch acrobats perform.

What it means

Other people's fears will keep you from a risky plan.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • You do the tricks yourself — you will have to answer for a scandal while rivals mock you.
  • Female acrobats — someone may spread nasty rumours about you.
