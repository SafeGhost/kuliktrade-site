Laundry in a dream

You do the laundry.

What it means

There will be a struggle, but in the end you will win prosperity.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • If the clothes come out clean, your efforts will bring full happiness.
  • If they don't, money won't bring joy.
  • A laundry worker calling at your home tells you to look after your health and your valuables.
