A pipe in a dream

You smoke a pipe.

What it means

An old friend will visit, and old disagreements will be settled peacefully.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Seeing pipes — peace and comfort after long struggle.
  • An old, broken pipe — stagnation in your affairs; take care of your health.
