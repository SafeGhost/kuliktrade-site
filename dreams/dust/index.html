Dust in a dream

You are covered in dust.

What it means

Other people's mistakes will hurt your affairs a little.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • If you manage to brush the dust off sensibly, you will make up the loss.
  • For a young woman, the dream advises caring for the relationship: her beloved may be drawn to someone else.
