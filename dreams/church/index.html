A church in a dream

You see a church.

What it means

A pleasure you have long looked forward to may disappoint you.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Entering a gloomy church — a sad occasion and low spirits.
  • A chapel — disagreements among friends and changes at work.
  • Young people entering a chapel — beware of insincere love.
