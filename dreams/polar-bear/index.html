A polar bear in a dream

You see a polar bear.

What it means

Trouble may come in a pleasant disguise: enemies will pretend to be friends.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Rivals will try to take your place.
  • A polar bear skin — you will successfully overcome any opposition.
