Laziness in a dream

You feel too lazy to do anything.

What it means

You may make a mistake when starting something new and feel bitterly let down.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • For a young woman who sees her partner as lazy, the dream advises warmth: coldness drives serious people away.
