An axe in a dream

You see an axe.

What it means

Your joys will depend on your own drive and energy.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Others using an axe — lively, active friends will be around you.
  • For a young woman — her chosen one will be worthy, though not rich.
  • A rusty or broken axe — take care of your health and money.
