A painting in a dream

You look at paintings.

What it means

Stay alert: someone around you may deceive you or bear you ill will.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Painting a picture — a venture that will not pay.
  • Destroying paintings — you will be forgiven for standing up for yourself harshly.
  • Buying paintings — empty investments.
  • Being surrounded by masterpieces — you will long for ever higher goals, and today’s success will seem small.
