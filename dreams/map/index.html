A map in a dream

You study a map.

What it means

You will plan changes in your affairs, and despite small letdowns they will pay off.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Looking for a map means discontent with your situation will give you fresh energy and lead you to something better.
  • For a young woman, it promises a rise through her own drive.
