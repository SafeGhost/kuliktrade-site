Knitting in a dream

You are knitting.

What it means

A quiet, peaceful home awaits you, where loved ones care for you and make you happy.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • A young woman knitting: a quick and happy wedding.
  • Being in a knitting mill: thrift and steady growth.
  • A run-down mill: setbacks in work and love.
