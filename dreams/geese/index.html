Geese in a dream

You see geese swimming.

What it means

Your wealth is slowly growing.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Geese on the grass: sure success.
  • Plucking geese: coming into property.
  • Eating goose: someone may dispute what belongs to you.
  • Dead geese: losses and annoyance.
