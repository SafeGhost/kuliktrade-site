A giant in a dream

A giant suddenly appears in front of you.

What it means

A serious struggle with rivals lies ahead.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • If the giant runs away from you: prosperity and good health.
  • If he blocks your way: a rival may get the upper hand, so be patient.
