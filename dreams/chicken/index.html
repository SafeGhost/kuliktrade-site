A chicken in a dream

You see a brood of chicks.

What it means

You will have many worries, but some of them will turn to your advantage.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Half-grown chickens — a lucky venture if you put real effort into it.
  • Hens going to roost — rivals are plotting something.
  • Eating chicken — selfishness may spoil your good name.
