A doctor in a dream

You see a doctor.

What it means

Any worries or ailments you have will soon pass.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • The doctor looks anxious — your trials may drag on, so take care of yourself.
  • For a young woman, the dream advises not to waste her energy and beauty on empty amusements.
