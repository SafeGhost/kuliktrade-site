Revenge in a dream

You take revenge on someone.

What it means

Resentment is pushing you toward harsh acts that could cost you friends.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Taking revenge yourself: keep your anger in check or it will bring trouble.
  • Someone takes revenge on you: watch out for enemies.
