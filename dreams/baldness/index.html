Baldness in a dream

You see a bald man.

What it means

Smooth operators will try a deal against you, but if you stay alert you will outsmart them.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • A bald baby — a happy home, a loving partner and good children.
  • For a young woman, a bald man — think hard about the next marriage proposal.
  • A bare, bald hill — hard times.
