Singing in a dream

You hear singing.

What it means

High spirits, cheerful friends and good news from far away await you.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Singing yourself while everything promises happiness: someone's envy will creep into your joy.
  • A sad song: your affairs will take an unexpected turn.
