Laughter in a dream

You laugh and feel cheerful.

What it means

Your plans will succeed, and you will have bright company.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Hearing children laugh happily means joy and good health.
  • Laughing wildly at something strange means discord around you.
  • Laughing at someone's misfortune warns you not to hurt friends for your own gain.
  • Hearing mocking laughter means setbacks and feeling unwell.
