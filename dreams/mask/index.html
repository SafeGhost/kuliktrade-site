A mask in a dream

You put on a mask.

What it means

Your good intentions will be misread, but the temporary distance will work in your favor.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Seeing a mask means someone may be unfaithful to you.
  • Others in masks mean you will have to fight lies and envy.
  • Going to a masquerade warns you not to chase empty fun at the expense of work and home.
  • For a young woman, the dream advises modesty.
