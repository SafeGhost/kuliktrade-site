A suitcase in a dream

You pack your suitcase.

What it means

You will soon go on a pleasant trip.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Belongings scattered from a trunk mean quarrels and a hasty, joyless trip.
  • An empty trunk means disappointment in love.
  • Being unable to open it means missing a good chance.
  • Being unable to close it means a hoped-for trip will fall through.
