A dog in a dream

A dog nuzzles up to you.

What it means

Great gains and loyal friends.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • A vicious dog means enemies and trouble.
  • A dog biting you warns of a quarrelsome partner in marriage or business.
  • A friendly white dog promises success in work and love.
  • A dog following you on a journey means steadfast friends and good fortune.
  • A dog howling foretells a long separation from friends.
