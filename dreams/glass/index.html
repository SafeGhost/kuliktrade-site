Glass in a dream

You look through a pane of glass.

What it means

Be ready for some hopes not to come true right away.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Seeing clearly through a window pane: you'll find work, but under someone else's direction.
  • Cloudy glass: an awkward position.
  • Breaking glass dishes or a window: an unlucky end to something you started.
  • Receiving cut glass as a gift: you'll be admired for your intelligence and talent.
