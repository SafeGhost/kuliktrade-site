A field in a dream

You see a green field.

What it means

A green or ripe field promises great abundance and happiness.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • A freshly ploughed field: income will rise soon and you will earn respect.
  • A field ready for sowing: your long efforts will soon pay off.
  • A dry, harvested field: bleak prospects.
