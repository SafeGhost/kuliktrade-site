A spider in a dream

You see a spider.

What it means

Careful, steady work will bring you a good income.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • A spider spinning its web means peace and security at home.
  • Killing one means a quarrel with your partner.
  • A spider bite warns of disloyalty and rivals at work.
  • Many spiders in webs around you mean luck, health and true friends.
