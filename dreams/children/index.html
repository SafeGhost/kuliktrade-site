Children in a dream

You see beautiful children.

What it means

Prosperity and plenty of joy await you.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Children studying or working — calm and comfortable times.
  • Playing with children — success in your affairs and in love.
  • Upset children — beware of people who only pretend to be friends.
  • Your child seems unwell in the dream — it asks you to give them more attention and warmth.
