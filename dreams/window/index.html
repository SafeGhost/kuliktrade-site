A window in a dream

You see windows.

What it means

Not all your bright hopes will come true; be ready for disappointment.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Closed windows point to loneliness.
  • Broken ones mean suspicions of disloyalty.
  • Climbing into a house through a window means your unfair methods will come to light.
  • Sitting on a window sill warns of a foolish act.
