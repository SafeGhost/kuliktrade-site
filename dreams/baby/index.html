A baby in a dream

You see a clean, happy baby.

What it means

Love returned and many warm friends are waiting for you.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • A crying baby — save your strength; small upsets are possible.
  • A baby walking alone — independence.
  • For a woman, nursing a baby — do not trust blindly, even those closest to you.
