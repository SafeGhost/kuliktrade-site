Poison in a dream

You have been poisoned.

What it means

You will soon feel someone’s painful influence.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Throwing the poison away — by sheer will you will change an unpleasant situation.
  • A rival or enemy is poisoned — you will overcome obstacles.
  • Your loved ones are poisoned — expect harm from an unexpected side.
  • Recovering from poison — success after worry.
