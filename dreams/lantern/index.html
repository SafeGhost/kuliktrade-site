A lantern in a dream

You see a lantern moving ahead of you in the dark.

What it means

Unexpected wealth is on its way.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • If the lantern suddenly disappears, luck will turn against you.
  • Carrying a lantern means your kindness will win many friends.
  • If it goes out, the position you want will stay out of reach for now.
  • Tripping and breaking it means helping others may cost you your own place.
  • Cleaning a lantern means great chances lie ahead.
  • Buying one means lucky deals.
