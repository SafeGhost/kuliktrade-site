A man in a dream

You see a handsome, well-built man.

What it means

You will enjoy life to the full and become wealthy.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • An ugly, sour-faced man means disappointments and tangled affairs.
  • For a woman, a handsome man foretells honors, while an ugly one means trouble from a false friend.
