A candle in a dream

You see a candle burning with a clear, steady flame.

What it means

The people around you are loyal, and your wellbeing rests on solid ground.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • For a young woman, lighting a candle means secret meetings with her sweetheart.
  • A candle guttering in a draught means enemies are spreading rumours about you.
  • Putting out a candle means sad news about friends.
