A piano in a dream

You see a piano.

What it means

A joyful occasion lies ahead.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Beautiful music playing — success and good health.
  • Off-key music — annoying problems to deal with.
  • A sad melody — sad news.
  • A broken or out-of-tune piano — dissatisfaction with yourself.
  • Playing a hard but lovely piece — you will win the heart of someone who was indifferent.
