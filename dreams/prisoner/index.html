A prisoner in a dream

You see convicts.

What it means

Seeing convicts means troubles and sad news.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Being a convict yourself — one matter will worry you, but you will sort it all out.
  • For a young woman seeing her sweetheart in prison clothes — she should take a closer look at his feelings.
