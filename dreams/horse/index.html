A horse in a dream

You ride a horse.

What it means

Prosperity and a life full of joy are waiting for you.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • A white horse: good luck and pleasant company.
  • A horse throws you: a strong rival will appear.
  • A runaway horse: someone else's folly will hurt you.
  • Catching and saddling a horse: everything you do will go up.
