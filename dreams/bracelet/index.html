A bracelet in a dream

You wear a bracelet, a gift from a loved one or a friend.

What it means

A wedding soon, or a strong and happy union.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Losing a bracelet means small losses and annoyance.
  • Finding one means a valuable gain.
