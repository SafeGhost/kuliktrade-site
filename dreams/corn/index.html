Corn in a dream

You walk through a green cornfield heavy with ears.

What it means

Wealth, a rich harvest in all your affairs and peace at home await you.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Ripe corn — fame and plenty.
  • Watching others harvest corn — you will rejoice in friends' success.
  • Eating sweet corn — harmony among friends.
  • Withered ears — disappointments.
