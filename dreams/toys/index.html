Toys in a dream

You see new toys.

What it means

New, unbroken toys mean joy in the family.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Broken toys mean sorrow at home.
  • Children playing with toys mean a happy marriage.
  • Giving toys away means acquaintances may ignore you.
