Bedbugs in a dream

You see bedbugs.

What it means

Expect lingering troubles and low energy; take care of your health.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • A swarm of bedbugs means a stretch of worry.
  • Crushing them brings a scare that turns out to be groundless.
  • Scalding them with boiling water means difficulties you will overcome.
  • Little bugs everywhere point to annoying household hassles.
