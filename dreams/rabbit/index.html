A rabbit in a dream

You see rabbits.

What it means

Things will turn for the better, and your gains will please you more than before.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • White rabbits — faithfulness in love, whether married or single.
  • Rabbits playing — children will bring you much joy.
