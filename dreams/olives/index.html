Olives in a dream

You pick olives with cheerful friends.

What it means

Your affairs will turn out well, and pleasant surprises await you.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Eating olives means contentment and loyal friends.
  • Taking them from a jar promises a merry gathering.
  • Breaking a jar of olives warns of disappointment on the eve of a celebration.
