A quilt in a dream

You see a quilt.

What it means

Comfort and well-being lie ahead.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • A clean quilt — your practical good sense will win the respect of someone dear to you.
  • A quilt with holes — your partner will value you, though you dreamed of someone else.
  • A dirty quilt — take care of your appearance and manners; it matters in love.
