A castle in a dream

You are inside a castle.

What it means

You will have enough means to live as you wish, and you may travel a lot.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • An old, ivy-covered castle means you will lean towards romance; do not rush into an engagement.
  • Leaving a castle means losing something valuable, so guard what is yours.
