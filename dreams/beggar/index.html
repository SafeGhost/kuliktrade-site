A beggar in a dream

You see an old beggar.

What it means

Time to sort out your money: without care you could lose a lot.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Giving to a beggar shows you feel cramped in your present life and want change.
  • Refusing a beggar is a bad sign: be more generous.
  • Gossip may be going around, so guard your reputation.
