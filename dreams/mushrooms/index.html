Mushrooms in a dream

You see mushrooms.

What it means

Don't rush to get rich at any cost: quick money may vanish in lawsuits and idle pleasures.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Eating mushrooms warns of an embarrassing situation and a dubious affair.
  • For a young woman, the dream advises not to chase frivolous pleasures.
