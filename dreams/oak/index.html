An oak in a dream

You see a grove of oaks.

What it means

Great prosperity in every part of life awaits you.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • An oak full of acorns promises growth and promotion.
  • An oak split by lightning warns of sudden shocks.
  • For sweethearts, the dream promises a happy start to life together soon.
