A road in a dream

You walk along a road.

What it means

A road lined with trees and flowers brings pleasant, unexpected luck.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • A rough, unknown road: a new venture will bring more trouble than gain.
  • Walking with friends: a happy home and family.
  • Losing your way: a mistake in a work decision.
