A quarrel in a dream

You are quarreling.

What it means

The dream foretells unhappiness and heated arguments.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • For a young woman — a serious falling-out.
  • For a couple — long disagreements; be patient with each other.
  • Hearing others quarrel — sluggish business.
