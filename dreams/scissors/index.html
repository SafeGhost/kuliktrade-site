Scissors in a dream

You see a pair of scissors.

What it means

Jealousy, reproaches and quarrels between partners are possible, and work may stall.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Sharpening scissors: you will have to do a task you dislike.
  • Breaking them: a quarrel and maybe a breakup.
  • Losing them: you will try to dodge dull chores.
