Candy in a dream

You are eating fresh, crunchy candy.

What it means

Cheerful gatherings, flirting and love.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Making candy means hard work will pay off.
  • Sour candy means annoyance over secrets kept too long.
  • Receiving a box of chocolates means compliments and prosperity.
  • Sending one means your proposal may be turned down.
