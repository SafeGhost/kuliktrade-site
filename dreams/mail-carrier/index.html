A mail carrier in a dream

A mail carrier brings you letters.

What it means

Unwelcome news will come soon.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Hearing the mail carrier's whistle means an unexpected visitor.
  • A mail carrier passing by without your mail means disappointment and sadness.
  • Handing letters to him warns of envy.
  • Chatting with a mail carrier warns you not to get drawn into shady affairs.
