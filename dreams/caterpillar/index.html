A caterpillar in a dream

You see a caterpillar.

What it means

Hypocrites are near you; do not trust fine words.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Setbacks in love or work are possible.
  • You may be put in an awkward spot that brings little honour or gain.
