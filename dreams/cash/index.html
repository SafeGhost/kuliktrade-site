Cash in a dream

You have plenty of cash, but all of it is borrowed.

What it means

People will respect you, but those close to you will find you calculating and cold; be warmer to others.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • For a young woman, spending borrowed money means a deception will come to light and she may lose a dear friend.
