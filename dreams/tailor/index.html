A tailor in a dream

You see a tailor.

What it means

An upcoming trip will bring some fuss. Prepare in advance.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Arguing with a tailor means a plan won't meet expectations.
  • A tailor taking your measurements means arguments and disagreements.
