Running in a dream

You are running with others.

What it means

A celebration is coming, and your affairs will move toward success.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Running alone: you will outpace everyone in wealth and standing.
  • Stumbling or falling: guard your property and reputation.
  • Running from danger: a risk of losses and worry.
