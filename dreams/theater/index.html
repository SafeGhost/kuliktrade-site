A theater in a dream

You are at the theater.

What it means

New friends will bring you much joy, and your affairs will go well.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Acting on stage means short-lived joy.
  • An opera means your wishes will come true.
  • Escaping a theater during a fire or panic means a risky venture.
