An escape in a dream

You escape from harm or an accident.

What it means

Usually a good dream: danger will pass you by.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Escaping from confinement means hard work will lift you up in life.
  • Trying to escape and failing means enemies will try to slander and cheat you.
