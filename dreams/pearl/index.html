A pearl in a dream

You see pearls.

What it means

Good business and pleasant social occasions lie ahead.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Your beloved gives you pearls — celebrations, joy and a faithful partner free of jealousy.
  • Losing or breaking pearls — sadness and misunderstandings.
  • Admiring pearls — you pursue love and wealth with honest intentions.
