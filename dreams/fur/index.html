Fur in a dream

You are dressed in fur.

What it means

Want and hardship will pass you by.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Seeing fine fur: honour and wealth.
  • Trading in furs: prosperity and many interesting ventures.
  • For a young woman, wearing luxurious fur means marrying a wise person.
