A lie in a dream

You tell a lie to escape punishment.

What it means

You may treat an innocent person unfairly.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Lying to protect a friend means unfair criticism, but you will rise above it.
  • Hearing others lie means someone is trying to trap you.
  • Being called a liar means trouble from deceitful people.
