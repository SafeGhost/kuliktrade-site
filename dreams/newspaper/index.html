A newspaper in a dream

You read a newspaper.

What it means

Be honest in your dealings: any slip may come to light and hurt your reputation.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Printing a newspaper promises trips abroad and new friends.
  • Trying but failing to read a newspaper foretells failure in a shaky venture.
