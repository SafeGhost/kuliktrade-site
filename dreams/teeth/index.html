Teeth in a dream

Your teeth are falling out.

What it means

This warns of unpleasant news and hardships. Look after your health and keep an eye on your affairs.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Loose teeth mean setbacks and gloomy news.
  • Getting fillings means you'll recover something lost.
  • Admiring your white, beautiful teeth means happiness and wishes come true.
  • Being toothless means it's hard to move ahead, so take care of yourself; others without teeth mean enemies are slandering you in vain.
