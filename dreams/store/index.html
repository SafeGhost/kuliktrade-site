A store in a dream

You are in a store full of goods.

What it means

Wealth and progress are coming your way.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • An empty store means failure and quarrels.
  • Selling in a store means a quick rise thanks to your energy and friends' help.
  • A big department store means income from many sources.
