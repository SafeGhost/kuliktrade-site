Swearing in a dream

You are swearing.

What it means

Annoying obstacles will appear at work. Watch your words.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Swearing in front of your family means trouble at home caused by your own behavior.
  • For someone in love, it advises not to give in to groundless suspicion.
