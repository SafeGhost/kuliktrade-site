A crow in a dream

You see a crow.

What it means

A crow foretells bad luck and grief.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Hearing crows caw — someone will talk you into a bad deal over property; don't give in.
  • For a young man — beware of cunning temptations.
