A storm in a dream

You see a storm coming.

What it means

A hard stretch is ahead: health worries, setbacks at work, time apart from friends. Save your strength.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • If the storm passes by, the trouble will be lighter than it seemed.
  • A sudden squall means disappointment at work.
  • A raging tempest means friends may grow distant for a while.
