Snow in a dream

You see snow.

What it means

There will be no real misfortune, but things may move slowly and your mood may dip.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Caught in a snowstorm: a long-awaited joy may be delayed.
  • Dirty snow: you will swallow your pride and make peace.
  • Melting snow: your fears will turn to joy.
