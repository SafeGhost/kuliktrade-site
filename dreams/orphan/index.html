An orphan in a dream

You comfort orphans.

What it means

Others' troubles will move you, and you will give up some of your own pleasures for them.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • If the orphans are your relatives, new duties will enter your life and you will drift apart from friends for a while.
