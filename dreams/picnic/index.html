A picnic in a dream

You are at a picnic.

What it means

Success and real enjoyment lie ahead.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • For young people this dream promises pure happiness.
  • A storm or some trouble spoils the picnic — luck in work or love will be briefly postponed.
