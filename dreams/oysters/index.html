Oysters in a dream

You eat oysters.

What it means

Don't let a thirst for pleasure and profit override your conscience.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Seeing oysters promises a comfortable life and a big family.
  • Selling oysters means you will be too pushy in chasing love or money.
