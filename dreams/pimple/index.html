A pimple in a dream

You have pimples on your skin.

What it means

Small worries will bother you.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Others with pimples — complaints and fuss from other people.
  • Pimples spoiling your looks — acquaintances will gossip about your behavior.
  • These are only small annoyances; do not take them to heart.
