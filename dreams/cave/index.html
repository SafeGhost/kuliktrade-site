A cave in a dream

A cave yawns before you in the moonlight.

What it means

Doubts and obstacles from rivals lie ahead; save your strength and look after your health.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Being inside a cave means change and a possible cooling with people dear to you.
  • For a young woman, walking in a cave with a friend means do not trust a charming but unreliable person.
