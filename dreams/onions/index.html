Onions in a dream

You see a lot of onions.

What it means

Your success will stir envy, but you will overcome it.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Eating onions means you will overcome all resistance.
  • Onions growing in a bed mean some rivalry, just enough to keep things interesting.
  • Cooked onions promise calm and a small income.
  • Cutting onions until your eyes water warns that rivals may get the upper hand.
