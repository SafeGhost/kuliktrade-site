A whale in a dream

You see a whale approaching a ship.

What it means

You will have to choose between different duties, and your property may be at risk.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • If the whale is defeated, you will make the right choice and succeed.
  • A whale overturning a ship points to a string of troubles.
