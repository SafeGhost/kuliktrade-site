Peas in a dream

You eat peas.

What it means

Good health and growing wealth lie ahead.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Peas growing — lucky ventures.
  • Planting peas — your hopes are well founded and will come true.
  • Picking peas — you will enjoy the fruits of your work.
  • Canned peas — your hopes will hang in doubt for a while, but luck will set them free.
  • Dried peas — you are overloading yourself.
