A keyhole in a dream

You peep through a keyhole.

What it means

You could hurt someone by giving away a secret. Keep what you are told to yourself.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Catching others peeping through a keyhole: false friends are poking into your affairs for their own gain.
  • Not finding the keyhole: you may hurt a friend without meaning to.
