Stars in a dream

You look at bright stars.

What it means

Good health and well-being lie ahead.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • A shooting star means sadness.
  • Stars that appear and vanish mean strange changes.
  • A star falling on you means worries in the family.
