Beer in a dream

You are drinking beer in a bar.

What it means

Disappointments are ahead: not everything will go as you hope.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Watching others drink means a schemer may get in the way of your best hopes.
  • If the beer is fresh and tasty and the setting pleasant, things will work out.
