A camp in a dream

You are camping in a tent under the open sky.

What it means

Expect changes in your affairs and get ready for a long, tiring trip.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Seeing a campsite means friends will move away and your own prospects will dim for a while.
  • For a young woman, being in a camp means her partner will wait long for her yes but will prove kind.
  • A military camp warns against rushing into love.
