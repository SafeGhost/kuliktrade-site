A bank in a dream

You receive money at a bank.

What it means

Good profit and prosperity are waiting for you.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Piles of banknotes — rising income and respect.
  • Handing money out yourself — do not be careless.
  • Empty teller windows — losses in your affairs.
