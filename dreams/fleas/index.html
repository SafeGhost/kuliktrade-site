Fleas in a dream

You see fleas.

What it means

Someone close to you may scheme and provoke you; don't take the bait.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • For a woman, being bitten by fleas warns of gossip from false friends.
  • Fleas on a partner point to his fickleness.
