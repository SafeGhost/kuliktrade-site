Eggs in a dream

You find a nest full of eggs.

What it means

Solid wealth and family happiness.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Eating eggs means unrest at home.
  • Fresh broken eggs mean fate is preparing generous gifts.
  • Rotten eggs mean losses.
  • Bird eggs promise an unexpected profit.
