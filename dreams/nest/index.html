A nest in a dream

You see a bird's nest.

What it means

You will take up a venture that brings success.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • For a young woman, the dream foretells a move.
  • An empty nest means sadness while a friend is away.
  • A hen's nest promises joys at home and cheerful, obedient children.
  • A nest with broken or rotten eggs foretells disappointment.
