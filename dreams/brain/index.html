A brain in a dream

You see your own brain.

What it means

Unfriendly surroundings are irritating you and souring your mood; look for a place where you feel at ease.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Seeing an animal's brain means inner worries.
  • Eating brains means new knowledge and unexpected profit.
