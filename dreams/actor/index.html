An actor in a dream

You see an actress.

What it means

Your life will be full of pleasure and good luck for now.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • You are an actor yourself — you will work hard, but gladly.
  • Falling for an actor or actress — you lean more toward fun than hard work.
  • A performer in trouble — you will gladly help a friend out of debt.
  • For a young woman, marrying an actor — regret will follow the glamour.
