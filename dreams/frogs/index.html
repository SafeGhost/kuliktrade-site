Frogs in a dream

You see frogs in the grass.

What it means

You'll have a calm, even-tempered friend who gives sound advice.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Catching frogs: don't neglect your health.
  • Frogs in a marsh: trouble you'll overcome with the help of kind people.
  • Hearing frogs croak: a visit to friends that won't bring much.
