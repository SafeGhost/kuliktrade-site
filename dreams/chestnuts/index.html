Chestnuts in a dream

You hold chestnuts.

What it means

You may lose something in your affairs, but you will have a pleasant, loyal life partner.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Eating chestnuts — a short spell of sadness, then happiness.
  • For a young woman, the dream promises a well-off suitor and comfort.
