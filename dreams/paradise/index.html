Paradise in a dream

You find yourself in paradise.

What it means

You have loyal friends ready to help.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • The dream brings good hopes to those about to set off on a long journey.
  • To parents it promises fine, obedient children.
  • If you are ill or in trouble, it foretells a quick recovery and luck.
  • To lovers it promises wealth and faithfulness.
  • Setting off for paradise and getting lost warns that a tempting venture may disappoint.
