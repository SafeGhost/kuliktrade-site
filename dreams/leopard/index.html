A leopard in a dream

A leopard attacks you.

What it means

The future looks bright, but trusting the wrong people will bring trouble.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Killing a leopard means winning in your affairs.
  • A caged leopard means enemies will surround you but fail to harm you.
  • A leopard running from you means you will overcome trouble in work or love by persistence.
  • A leopard skin warns of a charming cheat.
