An altar in a dream

You see an altar in a temple.

What it means

The altar warns you against a mistake and calls you to admit when you are wrong.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • A priest at the altar — disputes and trouble at home and at work.
  • A wedding at the altar — sorrow among your friends.
