A guitar in a dream

You play the guitar.

What it means

A merry gathering, serious courtship and harmony at home lie ahead.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • An out-of-tune or broken guitar: disappointment in love.
  • Hearing strange guitar music: beware of flattery and sweet talk.
