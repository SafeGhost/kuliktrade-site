Medicine in a dream

You take medicine.

What it means

If it tastes pleasant, trouble will come but soon turn out for your good.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Bitter, nasty medicine means a long stretch of sorrows, so look after your health.
  • Giving medicine to others warns you not to abuse someone's trust.
