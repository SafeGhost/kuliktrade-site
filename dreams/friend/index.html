A friend in a dream

You see your friends healthy and happy.

What it means

Good news from them, or a meeting with them, is coming soon.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • A friend looking worn out may need your help.
  • A friend standing like a statue on a hill: you will move ahead yet keep your convictions.
  • Shaking hands with someone who wronged you as they leave sadly: a rift with a close friend.
