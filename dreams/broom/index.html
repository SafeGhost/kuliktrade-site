A broom in a dream

You see a new broom.

What it means

Thrift and a quick improvement in your fortunes.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • A broom in use means do not gamble with money; risky investments will lead to losses.
  • Losing a broom means disorder and discord at home.
