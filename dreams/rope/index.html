A rope in a dream

You see a rope.

What it means

Work and love will get tangled, but you have the strength to sort it all out.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Climbing a rope: you will beat your enemies.
  • Climbing down: disappointment.
  • Being tied up: you will give in to feelings against your better judgment.
  • Breaking a rope: you will overcome rivals.
  • Walking a tightrope: a risky venture will surprisingly pay off.
