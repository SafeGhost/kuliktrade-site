A family in a dream

You see your family.

What it means

A close and cheerful family in a dream points to good health and an easy, comfortable life.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • If the family is quarrelling or someone is ill, expect a gloomy spell and disappointment; smooth things over early.
