Drowning in a dream

You are drowning.

What it means

Take care of your property and health: hard times lie ahead.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • If you are rescued, you will rise to wealth and honour.
  • If you save someone who is drowning, you will help a friend rise and find well-earned happiness yourself.
