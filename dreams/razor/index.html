A razor in a dream

You see a razor.

What it means

The dream foretells arguments and strife over troubles.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Cutting yourself with a razor — bad luck in a deal you are planning.
  • Fighting with a razor — someone will harass you almost beyond endurance.
  • A broken or rusty razor — unavoidable distress.
