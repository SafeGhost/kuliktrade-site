Silk in a dream

You are dressed in silk.

What it means

Your high hopes will be fulfilled, and people who fell out will make peace.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • For a young woman, old silk: pride in her family roots and the attention of a wealthy, older admirer.
  • Dirty or torn silk: guard your good name.
