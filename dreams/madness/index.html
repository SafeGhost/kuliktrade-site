Madness in a dream

You dream that you are going mad.

What it means

Hard times are ahead: look after your health so you don't lose what you have built.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Seeing others go mad means fickle friends and dashed hopes.
  • For a young woman, the dream advises not rushing her choice of partner.
