Garlic in a dream

You walk through a garlic patch.

What it means

You will rise from hardship to wealth and recognition.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Eating garlic: you'll take a sober, practical view of life.
  • For a young woman, the dream points to a marriage of convenience rather than love.
