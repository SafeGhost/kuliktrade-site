A coat in a dream

You see a new coat.

What it means

A new coat means recognition and honour for your work.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Wearing someone else's coat — you will have to ask a friend to vouch for you.
  • A torn coat — a close friendship cools and work turns dull.
  • Losing your coat — overconfidence will cost you dearly.
