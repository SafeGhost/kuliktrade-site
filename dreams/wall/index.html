A wall in a dream

A wall blocks your way.

What it means

Outside influences hold you back, and important matters may stall.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Jumping over the wall means you will overcome obstacles.
  • Breaking through it means persistence will win.
  • Pulling it down means you will defeat your opponents.
  • Building a wall means careful plans will make your living secure.
  • For a young woman, walking along the top of a wall promises lasting happiness.
