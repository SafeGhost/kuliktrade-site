An amputation in a dream

Your arm or leg is cut off.

What it means

You may lose something at work: a minor position or part of your income.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Losing a whole arm or leg — a slump in your affairs.
  • For a sailor — a storm.
  • A severed arm — discord between partners; watch out for deceit.
