A burn in a dream

You burn your hand on a clear fire.

What it means

Good news: your intentions are pure, and friends will support you.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Burning your feet walking over coals means you can do what others think impossible.
  • If the fire overcomes you, beware of betrayal by false friends.
