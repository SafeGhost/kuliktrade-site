A fever in a dream

You have a fever.

What it means

You are fretting over trifles while the best of life slips by; pull yourself together and do something worthwhile.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Seeing your relatives with a fever means small cares for your loved ones; surround them with kindness.
