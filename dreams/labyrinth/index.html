A labyrinth in a dream

You wander through a labyrinth.

What it means

Your affairs will get tangled, and home may feel tense.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • A dark labyrinth means a short but hard stretch of worries.
  • A labyrinth of green vines and trees brings unexpected joy where you feared loss.
  • A maze of railway tracks means long, tiring trips with interesting people but little profit.
