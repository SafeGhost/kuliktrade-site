A grasshopper in a dream

You see grasshoppers on green plants.

What it means

Someone may be threatening your interests; stay on guard.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Grasshoppers between you and the sun: you'll have an irritating problem to solve, but with care it will turn out in your favour.
  • Pointing grasshoppers out to others: you talk too openly about private matters.
