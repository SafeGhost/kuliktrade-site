Lightning in a dream

You see flashes of lightning.

What it means

Happiness and luck are coming, but they won't last long.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Lightning above your head means joy and gain.
  • Lightning striking nearby means gossip or trouble from a friend's luck.
  • Lightning striking you means sudden sorrows in work or love.
  • Lightning from black clouds means threats and losses, so keep a close eye on your affairs and loved ones.
