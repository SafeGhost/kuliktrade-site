A miser in a dream

You see a miser.

What it means

Selfishness will make true happiness hard to find, and love may disappoint you.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Being miserly yourself means your arrogance will push people away.
  • Friends turning out to be misers means others' nagging requests will wear you down.
  • For a woman helped by a miser, the dream promises love and wealth through her wits and tact.
