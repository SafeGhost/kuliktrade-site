Sheep in a dream

You see a flock of sheep.

What it means

Good times and shared prosperity await you.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Shearing sheep: a profitable stretch.
  • Thin, sick sheep: a promising plan may fall through.
  • Eating mutton: someone's rudeness will hurt you.
