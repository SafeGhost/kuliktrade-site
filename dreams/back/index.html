A back in a dream

You see a bare back.

What it means

Do not lend money or hand out advice right now: it may turn against you.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Someone turns their back and walks away — envy and jealousy are working against you.
  • Seeing your own back — save your strength and look after your health.
