Barefoot in a dream

You wander barefoot at night in torn clothes.

What it means

Your hopes may not come true, and things will be an uphill struggle.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Do not give up: the hard stretch will pass if you save your strength and do not rush.
