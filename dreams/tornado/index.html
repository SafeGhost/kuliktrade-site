A tornado in a dream

You find yourself in the path of a whirlwind.

What it means

A change is coming that may bring losses; meet it prepared.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • For a young woman, being caught in a wind that tugs at her clothes points to a secret flirtation that could turn into gossip.
