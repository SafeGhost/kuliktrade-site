Grass in a dream

You walk through lush green grass.

What it means

A very good dream: a happy life, fast-growing wealth, recognition and calm love.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Withered patches in the green grass: some tiredness or hitches at work.
  • Dry, dead grass: the opposite, luck turns away for now.
