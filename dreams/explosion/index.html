An explosion in a dream

You see an explosion.

What it means

The actions of people close to you will briefly upset you and cause losses.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • If your face is blackened by the blast, you will be unfairly accused of carelessness.
  • If you are thrown into the air, unworthy friends will abuse your trust.
