A ghost in a dream

You see a ghost.

What it means

Be careful with new acquaintances and don't rush into partnerships with strangers.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • The ghost of a late friend: a long trip in unpleasant company.
  • A ghost speaks to you: beware of a trap set by ill-wishers.
  • The ghost of someone living: keep your affairs under your own eye.
  • A female ghost floating across the sky: success in studies and unexpected wealth.
