Chess in a dream

You play chess.

What it means

Your affairs may stall and the company around you may feel dull; keep an eye on your health.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Losing the game — petty but annoying worries.
  • Winning — you will overcome whatever holds you back.
