A turkey in a dream

You see turkeys.

What it means

Good income awaits you, and a rich harvest if you farm.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Turkeys flying mean a quick rise from obscurity.
  • Sick turkeys mean hardships that will hurt your pride.
