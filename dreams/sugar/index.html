Sugar in a dream

You see sugar.

What it means

At home you may grow too picky and jealous for no reason. Value what you have.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Eating sugar means troubles that won't last and will end better than you expect.
  • Spilled sugar means a small loss.
