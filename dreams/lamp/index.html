A lamp in a dream

You see a lamp burning brightly.

What it means

A well-earned rise and happiness at home await you.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • A lamp full of oil means lively business.
  • An empty lamp means low spirits.
  • A dim light means jealousy and envy around you.
  • Lighting a lamp means a change that pays off.
  • Carrying a lamp means you will rely on yourself.
  • Dropping a lit lamp means plans suddenly collapse.
  • A broken lamp means worry about loved ones.
