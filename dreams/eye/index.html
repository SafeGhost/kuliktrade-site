An eye in a dream

You see someone's eye.

What it means

Ill-wishers are watching you closely, waiting for a chance to do harm.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • For someone in love, the dream says to stay alert: a rival may be close.
  • Brown eyes mean cunning, blue eyes indecision, grey eyes a love of flattery.
  • Sore eyes mean trouble.
