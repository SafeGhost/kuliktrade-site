A crossroads in a dream

You stand at a crossroads.

What it means

You may fail to hold on to a good opportunity you had before.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Not knowing which way to turn — small things will irritate you badly.
  • Choose your road firmly and luck will be on your side.
  • Soon you will face an important decision about work or love.
