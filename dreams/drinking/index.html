Drinking in a dream

You are drinking merrily in a noisy crowd.

What it means

Today's pleasures may later harm your good name.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • If you try to drink clear water but cannot, you will miss out on a pleasure someone keeps offering you.
