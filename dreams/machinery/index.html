Machinery in a dream

You see machines at work.

What it means

You will take on a hard project that brings worry but turns out well in the end.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Old machinery means enemies will get in the way of your progress.
  • Getting caught in machinery tells you to guard your money and avoid risky deals.
