Fire in a dream

You see fire.

What it means

Fire is a good sign if you don't get burned: it promises lasting well-being.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Lighting a fire: pleasant surprises and visits from afar.
  • Fighting flames: you will have to work hard for your wealth.
  • Seeing your home on fire: love and harmony in the family.
  • Getting burned: be careful.
