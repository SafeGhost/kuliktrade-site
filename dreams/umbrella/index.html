An umbrella in a dream

You walk under an umbrella.

What it means

Small troubles and annoyances are coming your way.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Others with umbrellas mean someone will ask you for help.
  • Borrowing one points to a misunderstanding with a friend.
  • Losing one means friction with someone you trust.
  • A broken umbrella warns of gossip.
  • A new umbrella in sunshine brings joy and plenty.
