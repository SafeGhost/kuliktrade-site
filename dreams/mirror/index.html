A mirror in a dream

You look at yourself in a mirror.

What it means

Many letdowns lie ahead, so take care of your health and money.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • A broken mirror means worry about loved ones.
  • Seeing others in a mirror means someone will treat you unfairly for their own gain.
  • Animals in a mirror mean disappointment and losses.
  • For a young woman, a happy partner in the mirror means a brief tiff, while a pale, weary one means a broken engagement.
