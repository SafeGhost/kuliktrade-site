A beard in a dream

You see a beard.

What it means

Someone you dislike will push their will on you, and the struggle may cost you money.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • A grey beard — bad luck and quarrels.
  • Someone pulls your beard — your property is at risk.
  • Combing and admiring your beard — success may go to your head; stay humble.
