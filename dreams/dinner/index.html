A dinner in a dream

You are one of many guests at a dinner.

What it means

Warm hospitality and pleasant attention from kind people await you.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Dining alone means you will have to think hard about the basics of life.
  • For a young woman, dinner with her beloved warns of a quarrel, unless it goes in perfect harmony; then it means joy.
