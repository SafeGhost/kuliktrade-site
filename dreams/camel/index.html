A camel in a dream

You see a camel.

What it means

You will have the patience and strength to get through even a very hard time.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Owning a camel means wealth.
  • A caravan of camels in the desert means help will come when hope seems almost gone, and you will recover against all odds.
