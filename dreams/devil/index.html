The devil in a dream

You see the devil.

What it means

Risky adventures lie ahead, and you will need some cunning to keep your good name.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Killing the devil: you will leave bad company and rise higher.
  • He comes as wealth or power: do not misuse your influence.
  • Shielding yourself from him: you will break free from selfish pleasures and help others.
