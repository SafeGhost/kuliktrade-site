The Pope in a dream

You speak with the Pope.

What it means

High honors await you.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Seeing the Pope without speaking to him — you will have to bow to someone else’s will.
  • The Pope looks sad or displeased — guard against bad habits and sorrow.
