A puddle in a dream

You step into a puddle.

What it means

There will be some annoyance, but later something good will come of it.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Muddy water — troubles will hang around for a while.
  • Getting your feet wet — today’s pleasure will cause harm later.
