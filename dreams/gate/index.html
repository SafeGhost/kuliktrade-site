A gate in a dream

You walk through a gate.

What it means

Worrying news about someone far away may come soon; work isn't going smoothly yet.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • A locked gate: present difficulties can't be overcome just yet.
  • Locking a gate yourself: successful ventures and loyal friends.
  • A broken gate: failure and discord.
  • Swinging on a gate: idle pastimes.
