A railroad in a dream

You see a railroad.

What it means

Keep a close eye on your affairs: someone wants your place.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Walking along the rails — skillful handling of your affairs will bring much happiness.
  • Walking on the ties — worry and hard work.
  • An obstacle on the tracks — someone is playing dirty.
  • For a young woman — a trip to visit friends and pleasant recognition.
