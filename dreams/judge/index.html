A judge in a dream

You stand before a judge.

What it means

A dispute will be settled by law and fairly.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • The case goes your way: the dispute will end well.
  • It goes against you: consider whether you are the one in the wrong, and set things right.
