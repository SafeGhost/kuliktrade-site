Hair in a dream

You see thick, well-groomed hair.

What it means

Neat, nicely styled hair means your affairs will improve.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Soft hair growing thick: happiness and comfort.
  • Tangled, uncombed hair: burdens at work and in relationships.
  • Having your hair cut: disappointments.
  • Snow-white hair: a long and fortunate life.
  • Flowers in your hair: coming troubles will prove less frightening than they look.
