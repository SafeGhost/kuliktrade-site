A splinter in a dream

You get a splinter.

What it means

Small annoyances from relatives or jealous rivals lie ahead.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • A splinter in your foot while visiting means an unpleasant visit.
  • Don't neglect small tasks or they will slowly go wrong.
