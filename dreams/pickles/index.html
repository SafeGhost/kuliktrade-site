Pickles in a dream

You eat pickles.

What it means

Without energy and good judgment you may waste effort on worthless pursuits.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Craving pickles — rivals appear, so keep your private life private.
  • Spoiled pickles — lovers’ quarrels.
