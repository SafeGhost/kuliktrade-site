A son in a dream

You see your son handsome and caring.

What it means

Your son will make you proud and achieve great things.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Your son hurt or unwell: worries ahead; pay more attention to your loved ones.
  • For a mother, seeing her son fall into a well: distress; rescuing him: the danger will pass unexpectedly.
