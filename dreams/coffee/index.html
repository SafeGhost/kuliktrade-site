Coffee in a dream

You drink coffee.

What it means

Friends will disapprove of your marriage plans, and at home there may be arguments.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Selling coffee — losses; buying it — your affairs stay sound.
  • Ground coffee — you will beat your troubles.
  • Green coffee beans — you have bold rivals, stay alert.
