Apricots in a dream

You see apricots on a tree.

What it means

The future looks rosy, but bitterness is hidden in it; do not get carried away too soon.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Eating apricots — a run of troubles.
  • Others eating them — you will not like the people around you.
  • Another reading: you are wasting time on trifles.
