A boat in a dream

You are sailing in a boat on clear water.

What it means

Bright prospects and a calm journey ahead.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Rough, stormy water means worries and unwelcome changes.
  • Boarding with a cheerful crowd and arriving safely brings luck and many pleasant gifts of fate.
  • Falling overboard in a storm means trouble.
