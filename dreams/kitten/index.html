A kitten in a dream

You see kittens.

What it means

Small annoyances will pester you. Do not let them pile up.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Chasing away or getting rid of a kitten: you will deal with these worries.
  • Snakes attacking kittens: rivals trying to harm you will harm themselves.
  • A woman seeing a fluffy white kitten: someone will try to deceive her, but her good sense will protect her.
