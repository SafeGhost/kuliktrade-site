A hotel in a dream

You are staying in a hotel.

What it means

Calm and income are waiting for you.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • A fine hotel: wealth and travel.
  • Owning a hotel: you will earn your fortune yourself.
  • Working in a hotel: you could find a better-paid job than the one you have.
  • A shabby, run-down inn: setbacks and gloomy trips.
