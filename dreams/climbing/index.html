Climbing in a dream

You climb a mountain.

What it means

If you reach the top, you will overcome the toughest obstacles on your way to success.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Failing to reach the top — your plans will need to change.
  • Climbing a ladder to the last rung — success at work; a ladder breaking — sudden difficulties.
  • Climbing up a house wall when a window opens to let you in — a bold plan will succeed even though friends disapprove.
