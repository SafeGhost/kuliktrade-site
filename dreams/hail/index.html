Hail in a dream

You are caught in a hailstorm.

What it means

Things you've started may not go as well as you'd like.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Hail falling through sunshine and rain: your worries won't last, and luck will soon smile on you.
  • For a young woman, the dream promises love after slights.
  • Hearing hail drumming on the roof: unsettling events.
