Tea in a dream

You brew tea.

What it means

You may act carelessly and regret it later. Think before you act.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Drinking tea with friends means usual fun will bore you and you'll want to help others.
  • Tea leaves at the bottom mean trouble in love.
  • Spilling tea means a household mess.
  • Craving tea means unexpected guests.
