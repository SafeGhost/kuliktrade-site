Gold in a dream

You hold gold in your hands.

What it means

You'll be unusually lucky in everything you take on.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Finding gold: your abilities will put you ahead.
  • Losing gold: through carelessness you could miss a great chance.
  • Finding a vein of gold: an honourable but troublesome role will come your way.
