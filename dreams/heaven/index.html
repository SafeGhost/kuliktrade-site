Heaven in a dream

You rise up to heaven.

What it means

A reward for your work may not bring the joy you expect. Look for happiness beyond success.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Climbing to heaven on a ladder: a big rise, though peace of mind will need finding on its own.
  • Being in paradise among friends: you will get through losses and make peace with them through wisdom.
  • Seeing the Heavenly City: your soul is calm, and troubles will barely touch you.
