A key in a dream

You find keys.

What it means

There will be peace at home and a lively turn at work.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Just keys: unexpected changes.
  • Losing keys: unpleasant adventures.
  • A broken key: a parting or jealousy.
  • A young woman unlocking a door with a key: a new admirer, but do not trust him too quickly.
