A hornet in a dream

You see a hornet.

What it means

An old friendship may crack and money may slip away. Protect both.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • A young woman stung by a hornet or caught in a hornets' nest: envious people will try to blacken her name in front of her admirers.
