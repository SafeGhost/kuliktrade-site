Feet in a dream

You look at your own feet.

What it means

Someone with a strong will may try to bend you to theirs; stand your ground.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Seeing other people's feet means you will defend your rights gently but firmly.
  • Washing your feet: others may take advantage of you.
  • Aching feet: awkward family quarrels.
