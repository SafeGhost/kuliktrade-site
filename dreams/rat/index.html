A rat in a dream

You see rats.

What it means

Beware of deceit and harm from neighbors; quarrels with companions are possible.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Catching rats — you will rise above others’ meanness and beat your enemies fairly.
  • Killing a rat — victory in any contest.
