A pig in a dream

You see a fat, healthy pig.

What it means

Your affairs will go sensibly and well.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Pigs wallowing in mud — beware of bad company and dubious deals.
  • For a young woman — a wealthy but jealous and greedy partner.
