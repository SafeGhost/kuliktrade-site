A carpet in a dream

You are walking on a carpet.

What it means

Prosperity and happiness.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Seeing a carpet means profit and wealthy friends to help you in need.
  • Buying carpets means great gain.
  • Selling them means a pleasant and profitable trip.
  • For a young woman, a carpet promises a beautiful, cosy home.
