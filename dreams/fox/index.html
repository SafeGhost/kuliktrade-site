A fox in a dream

You see a fox.

What it means

Beware of envious 'friends': someone is quietly harming your good name.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Chasing a fox: you are getting into dubious schemes and risky romances.
  • Killing a fox: you will win every contest.
