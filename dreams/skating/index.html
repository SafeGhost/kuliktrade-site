Skating in a dream

You are ice skating.

What it means

Guard your job and your valuables: there is a risk of losing something.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Falling through the ice: do not listen to bad advisers.
  • Watching others skate: people may spread gossip about you.
  • Young people on roller skates: good health and joy you bring to others.
