A door in a dream

You walk through a door.

What it means

Gossip follows you and enemies give you no peace.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Walking through the door of your childhood home means plenty and harmony.
  • A door that falls off its hinges and hurts someone tells you to be careful with the advice you give friends.
