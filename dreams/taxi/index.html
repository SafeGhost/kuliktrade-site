A taxi in a dream

You are riding in a taxi.

What it means

Pleasant pursuits and a decent living.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Riding in a taxi at night with others means you will have a secret to keep from friends.
  • Riding with a woman warns of gossip.
  • Driving a taxi yourself means hard work with little chance to advance.
