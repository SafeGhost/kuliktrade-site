A hawk in a dream

You see a hawk.

What it means

Cunning people will try to trick you. Keep an eye out for schemes around you.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Shooting a hawk means you will overcome obstacles after a long struggle.
  • A dead hawk means your rivals will be defeated.
  • Chasing a hawk away from chickens means rivals are close, but your care will bring luck at work.
