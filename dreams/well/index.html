A well in a dream

You draw water from a well.

What it means

Your heartfelt wishes will come true.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Muddy water in the well means unpleasantness.
  • Falling into a well points to despair; do not give in to it.
  • An empty well warns against trusting strangers or you may lose money.
  • A well with a pump brings new chances.
  • A spring gushing from the ground leads to knowledge and pleasure.
