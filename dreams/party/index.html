A party in a dream

You are having fun at a party.

What it means

You will see how much good there is in life.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • A party full of quarrels means your joy will be clouded.
  • A group of strangers attacking you for money warns that rivals will band together against you; if you get away unharmed, you will beat them in work and in love.
