A baptism in a dream

You see a baptism.

What it means

Be more restrained in arguments so as not to hurt your friends.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Being baptised yourself — do not give up who you are for other people's approval.
  • A baptism dream often speaks of a choice between duty to others and your own wishes.
