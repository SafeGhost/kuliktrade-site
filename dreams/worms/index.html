Worms in a dream

You see worms.

What it means

Shady people will weave petty intrigues against you.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • For a young woman, worms crawling on her point to being too down-to-earth; throwing them off means reaching for higher things.
  • Using worms as bait means you will cleverly turn your opponents' plans to your advantage.
