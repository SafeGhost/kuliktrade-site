Anger in a dream

You are angry and shouting.

What it means

A hard trial and a disappointment in someone close are ahead.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Friends or family are angry at you while you stay calm — you will reconcile the quarrelling sides and earn their gratitude.
