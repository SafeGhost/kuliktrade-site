A collision in a dream

You see a collision.

What it means

Take your time on the road and at work, and small troubles and letdowns will pass you by.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • For a young woman seeing a collision — she won't be able to choose between two admirers, and it will stir up quarrels.
