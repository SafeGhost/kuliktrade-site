A mustache in a dream

You have a mustache in the dream.

What it means

Pride and pushiness may harm you, so be more modest.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • For a woman, admiring a mustache warns of temptation: be careful.
  • For a man, shaving off his mustache means he will break with bad company and win back his good name.
