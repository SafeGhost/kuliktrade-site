A coffin in a dream

You see a coffin.

What it means

A hard stretch lies ahead: debts, setbacks at work and sadness at home.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • A coffin moving on its own — sorrow and joy will come mixed together.
  • Sitting on a coffin — look after your health and avoid quarrels; you will regret how you treated a friend.
