Liquor in a dream

You drink hard liquor.

What it means

Money may come in not quite the cleanest way, but cheerful friends will gather around you.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Buying liquor warns you not to claim what isn't yours.
  • Barrels of liquor mean prosperity, but less comfort at home.
  • Bottles mean good fortune in very real form.
  • Treating others means generosity even toward rivals.
