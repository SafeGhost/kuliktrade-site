A ribbon in a dream

You see bright ribbons.

What it means

Cheerful, pleasant people will be around you, and everyday worries will not weigh on you much.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • For a young woman, decking herself with ribbons foretells a proposal soon, if she avoids being careless.
  • Seeing other girls in ribbons means rivalry.
  • Buying ribbons means an easy, pleasant life.
