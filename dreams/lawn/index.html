A lawn in a dream

You walk across a well-kept lawn.

What it means

Reasons for joy and great success are coming.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • A cheerful party on a lawn means fun and successful dealings.
  • Waiting for a loved one on a green lawn means your dearest wishes will come true.
  • Dead grass and a soggy lawn mean quarrels and partings.
  • Snakes in the grass mean betrayal and spiteful hints.
