Oranges in a dream

You eat oranges.

What it means

Worries lie ahead: someone close will need your care, and there will be discontent at work.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Orange trees with ripe fruit promise health and well-being around you.
  • If the oranges are juicy and tasty, the troubles will be lighter.
  • Slipping on an orange peel warns of worries in the family.
