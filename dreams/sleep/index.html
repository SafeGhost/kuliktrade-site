Sleep in a dream

You are sleeping in a clean, fresh bed.

What it means

Peace and the goodwill of those you love await you.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Sleeping in an odd, uncomfortable place: look after your health; plans may fall through.
  • Sleeping next to a small child: family joy and love returned.
  • Watching others sleep: you will overcome every obstacle to win someone's heart.
