Warts in a dream

You have warts.

What it means

It will be hard to defend your good name against attacks.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Warts leaving your hands mean you will overcome obstacles on the way to prosperity.
  • Warts on others point to ill-wishers nearby.
  • Treating warts means you will vigorously ward off a threat to yourself and your loved ones.
