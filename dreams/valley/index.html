A valley in a dream

You walk through a green valley.

What it means

Your affairs will clearly improve, and love will bring harmony.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • A bare, barren valley means the opposite: stagnation and setbacks.
  • A marshy one points to worries and tiredness.
