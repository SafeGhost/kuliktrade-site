A uniform in a dream

You see someone in uniform.

What it means

Influential friends will help you get what you want.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • A young woman wearing a uniform will find love that is returned.
  • People in strange uniforms point to a falling-out between families or friends.
  • A loved one looking sad in uniform means a long time apart.
