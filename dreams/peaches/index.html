Peaches in a dream

You eat peaches.

What it means

Small disappointments are possible: work will give less than you expect, and planned trips may fall through.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Peaches on leafy trees mean you will get what you want, but with effort.
  • Dried peaches warn of people who may steal from you.
  • For a young woman, picking juicy peaches promises a wealthy, wise husband.
  • Green, hard peaches warn of coldness from relatives.
