Blindness in a dream

You have gone blind.

What it means

A sharp turn in your affairs is coming: protect what you have and avoid extra spending.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Seeing a blind person means someone worthy will ask you for help; do not refuse.
  • A blindfold means someone may deceive you, so check everything yourself.
