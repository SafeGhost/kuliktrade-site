A shirt in a dream

You are putting on a shirt.

What it means

Being unfaithful could push the one you love away.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Losing your shirt: trouble at work or in love.
  • A torn shirt: bad luck.
  • A dirty shirt: take care of your health.
