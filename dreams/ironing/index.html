Ironing in a dream

You are ironing clothes.

What it means

Home comforts and order in your affairs are waiting for you.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Burning your hands on the iron: look after your health and do not give in to jealousy.
  • Scorching the clothes: a rival will appear and stir up suspicion.
  • The iron is too cold: your home is short of warmth and affection.
