Jewels in a dream

You see jewels.

What it means

Joy and wealth are waiting for you.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Wearing jewels: high standing and dreams come true.
  • Finding jewels: quick and brilliant success.
  • Losing them: beware of flatterers.
  • Broken or tarnished jewellery: disappointment and friends who let you down.
