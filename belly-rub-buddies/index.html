Belly Rub Buddies · Kids & casual

Rub the tummy. Hear the giggle.

Cuddly pets for kids: give them belly rubs, feed them snacks, dress them up, and talk to them — they squeak your words right back in a funny little voice.

Why we made it

A pet game that is kind to kids and to parents.

The problem

Most pet games for kids are full of ads, timers that push you to pay, and pop-ups a five-year-old taps by accident.

Our answer

Six cuddly buddies, no ads, no tracking and no chat. Everything can be earned by playing, and every purchase sits behind a grown-up check.

What's inside

Lots to love.

Six buddies

Hammy the hamster, Capy the capybara, Bubbles the pygmy hippo, Mochi the red panda, Otto the sea otter — and a secret one.

Belly rubs & tickles

Tap the tummy, pat the head, tickle until they can't stop laughing. Each buddy has its own voice.

Talk to your buddy

Say something and your buddy repeats it in a squeaky voice. Processed only on the phone, never saved or sent.

Healthy choices

31 snacks and drinks. Each buddy loves some foods and refuses others, and too many sweets give a tummy ache.

Dress up

16 outfits, from party hats to a gold crown and glowing sneakers, plus cozy beds and four backgrounds.

Three mini-games

Snack catch, a road trip to Grandma's house, and Paw ball — bop the ball, pop the bubbles.
