Support

Write to support@kuliktrade.com — a real person answers, usually within a day.

My buddy is asleep and won't play

Buddies get sleepy after lots of belly rubs. Energy comes back by itself over time, or give a snack, a cup of cocoa or a Zoom fizz from the Shop.

Talk doesn't hear me

Talk needs microphone access. Allow it when the game asks, or in the phone's Settings › Apps › Belly Rub Buddies › Permissions › Microphone. Speak after the buddy says "I'm listening!". Your voice is never saved or sent.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the game's Settings and tap Restore. Buddies and the bundle come back on any phone signed in to the same Google Play (or Apple) account. Coin packs are used up when bought and can't be restored.

A grown-up question pops up when I tap a price

That's on purpose: every purchase needs a grown-up to answer a little math question first, so kids can't buy things by accident.

How do I turn off the music or vibration?

Settings in the game has switches for Sound, Music and Vibration.

How do I start over?

Settings › Start over. It asks a grown-up first and then clears all progress on this phone. Purchases can be restored afterwards.
