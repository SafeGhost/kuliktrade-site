Belly Rub Buddies

Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026 · KULIK TRADE CORP.

In short
  • Belly Rub Buddies collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts, no chat.
  • The microphone is used only while Talk is on. Your voice is never saved and never sent anywhere.
  • Game progress is stored only on the device.

The microphone (Talk)

When a child taps Talk, the app asks for permission to use the microphone. While Talk is on, the app listens for a short phrase, keeps it in the phone's memory for a few seconds, plays it back in a squeaky "buddy" voice and then discards it. The recording is never written to storage, never uploaded and never shared. Turning Talk off closes the microphone. You can deny or remove microphone access at any time in the phone's settings; the rest of the game works without it.

Game progress

Coins, buddies, outfits and settings are saved only on the device, inside the app's private storage. They are deleted when you use Settings › Reset in the game or uninstall the app.

Purchases

Optional one-time purchases are handled entirely by Google Play (and by the App Store on iPhone). Every purchase screen is behind a grown-up check. We never see payment details or account information. A list of items you own is kept on the device so the game can show them, and can be restored through the store with "Restore purchases".

Internet

The game runs offline. It uses the network only to talk to the store when a purchase is made or restored. The game contains no links to websites and no social features.

Data we collect

None. The app has no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services, and the developer receives no data from the app. Google or Apple may provide us with anonymous, aggregated store statistics and crash reports under their own privacy policies.

Children

Belly Rub Buddies is made for children and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the GDPR and Google Play's Families Policy: it does not collect, use or disclose personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. If the app ever needs to collect data, we will ask a parent for permission first.
