Place of Truth

Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026 · KULIK TRADE CORP.

In short
  • Place of Truth works entirely on your phone. It has no servers of its own and sends nothing to us. The only connection it makes is to the App Store or Google Play, when you buy something or restore a purchase.
  • The camera is used only during the rite: it notices your fingers on the lens and reads your heart rate from the light through them. No image is ever saved, and nothing is sent anywhere. The heart’s rhythm is kept on your phone until the end of the day, so the Oracle does not ask for it again, and is then replaced.
  • We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.
  • We, the developer, never receive any of your data.

Camera and flash

In the rituals of the Oracle, you place two fingers over the back camera. With your permission, the app turns on the flash and analyses the colour of the picture to notice that your fingers are there and to read your heart rate from the light through them. The rate is used together with the signs you choose to select your vision. It is never shown and never sent. It is kept on your phone only until the end of the day, so that you do not have to place your fingers again for another question that day; the next day it is replaced. The video frames are analysed in memory, one by one, and discarded at once. No photo or video is stored, and nothing is sent anywhere. The light turns off once your fingers have rested for a few seconds, or after 60 seconds if they have not. You can refuse the camera: the rite then listens to your finger held on the fire on the screen instead, and nothing is measured. Place of Truth sends no health data anywhere and is not a medical device.

What is kept on your phone

To remember your journey, the app keeps a few things in its own storage on your device:

  • your Scroll of Visions (the visions you received, the dreams you looked up and the notes you wrote);
  • the amulets you earned and the one you wear today;
  • which chapters of the Chronicle you have opened;
  • the rhythm of your heart for the current day only (see above);
  • in the Oracle Cards: the cards of your days and the spreads you read, your answers to “How did it go?”, the trials you passed, the lessons and seals of the Scribe’s Path, the signs you learned and, only if you enter it, your date of birth (used to find your birth star);
  • your settings: language, sound, vibration, music, hints and reminder time.

This information stays on your phone. It is removed when you delete the app. If you back up your phone (iCloud or a computer on iPhone, Google backup on Android), it is included in that backup the way the system backs up every app, so your journey can move with you to a new phone. The backup is kept by Apple or Google under their own policies: Apple’s Privacy Policy, Google’s Privacy Policy.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders and allow notifications, the app schedules a gentle morning reminder on your phone. It does not come on days you have already received your vision, and it falls silent by itself if you have not opened the app for two weeks. These are local notifications: they are created on your device and do not pass through any server. You can turn them off in the app's settings or in your phone’s settings.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) on iPhone and by Google (Google Play) on Android. We never see your name, account or payment details. To know what you have bought, the app asks the store on your device; the store answers with its own signed records.

Links

The page "Where this knowledge comes from" contains links to museums and libraries. They open in your browser only if you tap them; those websites have their own privacy policies.

Data we collect

None. Place of Truth contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking services, and the developer receives no data from the app. Apple and Google may provide us with anonymous, aggregated store statistics and crash reports only if you choose to share them with app developers in your phone’s settings.

Children

Place of Truth does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. If the app ever starts sending any of your data, we will tell you clearly in the app and ask for your permission first.
