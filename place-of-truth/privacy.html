Place of Truth

Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026 · KULIK TRADE CORP.

In short
  • Place of Truth works entirely on your phone. It does not connect to the internet.
  • The camera is used only during the rite: it notices your fingers on the lens and reads your heart rate from the light through them. No image and no heart rate is saved or sent.
  • We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.
  • We, the developer, never receive any of your data.

Camera and flash

In the rituals of the Oracle, you place two fingers over the back camera. With your permission, the app turns on the flash and analyses the colour of the picture to notice that your fingers are there and to read your heart rate from the light through them. The rate is used together with the signs you choose to select your vision. It is never shown, stored or sent. The video frames are analysed in memory, one by one, and discarded at once. No photo or video is stored, and nothing is sent anywhere. The light turns off once your fingers have rested for a few seconds, or after 60 seconds if they have not. Place of Truth collects no health data and is not a medical device.

What is kept on your phone

To remember your journey, the app keeps a few things in its own storage on your device:

  • your Scroll of Visions (the visions you received, the dreams you looked up and the notes you wrote);
  • the amulets you earned and the one you wear today;
  • which chapters of the Chronicle you have opened;
  • in the Oracle Cards: the cards of your days and the spreads you read, your answers to “How did it go?”, the trials you passed, the signs you learned and, only if you enter it, your date of birth (used to find your birth star);
  • your settings: language, sound, vibration, music and reminder time.

This information stays on your phone. It is removed when you delete the app. If you back up your phone (iCloud or a computer on iPhone, Google backup on Android), it is included in that backup the way the system backs up every app, under the backup provider’s own policy, for example Apple's Privacy Policy.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the app schedules a gentle morning reminder on your phone. These are local notifications: they are created on your device and do not pass through any server. You can turn them off in the app's settings or in your phone’s settings.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) on iPhone and by Google (Google Play) on Android. We never see your name, account or payment details. The app checks what you have bought using the store’s signed records on your device.

Links

The page "Where this knowledge comes from" contains links to museums and libraries. They open in your browser only if you tap them; those websites have their own privacy policies.

Data we collect

None. Place of Truth contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking services, and the developer receives no data from the app. Apple and Google may provide us with anonymous, aggregated store statistics and crash reports only if you choose to share them with app developers in your phone’s settings.

Children

Place of Truth does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. If the app ever starts sending any of your data, we will tell you clearly in the app and ask for your permission first.
