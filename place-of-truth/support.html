Common questions

The Oracle doesn't notice my fingers

Cover both the back camera and the flash with two fingers. Rest them lightly, without pressing, and keep still. If the light turns off, tap "Place your fingers again".

Is the camera recording me?

No. During the rite the camera only notices your fingers on the lens and reads your heart rate from the light through them. No image is saved, and nothing is sent anywhere. See the Privacy Policy.

I don't want to use the camera, or my phone has none

You don't need it. If you refuse the camera, or your phone has no flash, the rite asks you to hold your finger on the fire on the screen instead, and goes on as usual. You can allow the camera later in your phone's settings.

Will I keep my cards and seals on a new phone?

Yes, if your phone is backed up (iCloud on iPhone, Google backup on Android) and you restore that backup on the new phone. Purchases come back with "Restore Purchases" in any case.

Can it tell the future?

No. Place of Truth is for entertainment and reflection. The rituals are a modern reading inspired by real Egyptian texts. In Settings › Where this knowledge comes from, the app shows which parts come from ancient sources and which are its modern reading.

How do I get amulets?

Amulets are not sold one by one. The temple gives them for returning on different days and for completing rites. Open the casket in the top left corner to see which ones you have and how to receive the others. A yearly subscription also brings two rare amulets as a gift.

How do I change the language?

Tap the gear in the top right corner and choose a language. Place of Truth speaks English, Russian, Spanish, Portuguese, Turkish, German, French, Italian and Japanese.

How do I restore or cancel my purchase?

Restore: open Settings (the gear in the top right corner) › Full version › Restore Purchases. Cancel a subscription: on iPhone — Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Place of Truth; on Android — Google Play › your profile › Payments and subscriptions › Subscriptions › Place of Truth. On iPhone, cancel at least 24 hours before it renews; on Android, any time before it renews.
