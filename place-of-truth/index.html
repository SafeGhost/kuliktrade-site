“Place of Truth” was the village of the craftsmen who built the pharaohs’ tombs. From there came a dream book, a calendar of good and bad days, and questions to the god. Now this temple is open to you.

The Oracle’s vision of your day

Once a day, rest two fingers on the camera: the Oracle hears your heart. Then catch five signs flying out of the depths. Your heart, your signs and your birth star choose a vision that belongs only to you today.

Ask the Oracle

About love, family, work, prosperity, your path or yourself. Ask silently, and the answer comes as an image with a sign, a term and advice. The priest reads every vision for you.

The pharaohs’ dream book

Two hundred dreams with their meanings, many taken straight from a papyrus written down about 1275 BC, plus hundreds more from the classic dream book of Gustavus Miller. If a dream was bad, do what the Egyptians did: draw a protective sign and cover it with sand.

Good and bad days

Every day of the Egyptian year: is it good, bad or mixed, in the morning, at noon and in the evening, and what the papyrus advises.

Oracle Cards of the 36 stars

A deck of the 36 decans the Egyptians painted on the ceilings of royal tombs. A card of the day, the star you were born under, and spreads for love and work. Six lessons of the priest teach you to read the cards yourself.

Amulets, trials and the Chronicle

Twenty-four amulets the temple gives to those who come back, each with the story of who wore it and why. A new trial every day. And the Chronicle: the true history of the village, a new chapter every ten days.
